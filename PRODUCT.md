# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating, using, or contributing to Arai Hu's maintained open-software projects. Audience inference from the organization site's purpose; validate as the organization expands.

## Product Purpose

Present Arai Hu as an organization, explain its name, and direct visitors to its maintained projects in Portuguese, English, and Spanish.

## Positioning

Arai Hu is a small organization maintaining durable, open Go software across UI, documentation, workflow, and monitoring domains.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML generated by Go and templ; Cloudflare Workers serves assets and locale routes. English is the fallback. Project facts and links must remain accurate. No client runtime is required.

## Brand Commitments

The name Arai Hu is a free translation and interpretation of Tupi as “black cloud” / “nuvem negra.” The site must not claim an official linguistic translation or invent Indigenous symbolism.

## Evidence on Hand

Goshtoso, Manja, Pajé, and Xisnove are maintained projects with existing public sites. No customer testimonials, benchmarks, or commercial claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Put maintained projects before generic organization claims.
- Keep copy factual, brief, and localized.
- Prefer durable static delivery over application complexity.
- Make origin and project navigation legible in one visit.
