# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating, using, or contributing to Arai Hû's maintained open-software projects, plus designers and developers who need approved identity files and clear usage terms. Audience inference from the organization's present purpose; validate as it expands.

## Product Purpose

Present Arai Hû as an organization, explain its name, direct visitors to maintained projects, and publish one dependable source for approved identity assets and their usage terms in Portuguese, English, and Spanish.

## Positioning

Arai Hû is a small organization maintaining durable, open Go software across UI, documentation, workflow, and monitoring domains. Its public brand surface favors precise integration guidance over promotional claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML generated by Go and templ; Cloudflare Workers serves assets, canonical locale routes, and permanent aliases. English is the fallback and the authoritative language for license terms; Portuguese and Spanish license pages are informational translations. Project facts, release provenance, asset hashes, links, and identity geometry must remain accurate. No client runtime is required.

## Brand Commitments

The name Arai Hû is Guarani for “black cloud” or “dark cloud.” The site must not invent additional cultural claims or symbolism.

## Evidence on Hand

Goshtoso, Manja, Pajé, and X-9 are maintained projects with existing public sites. No customer testimonials, benchmarks, or commercial claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Put maintained projects before generic organization claims.
- Keep copy factual, brief, and localized.
- Prefer durable static delivery over application complexity.
- Make origin and project navigation legible in one visit.
- Make approved files, minimum sizes, clear space, prohibited uses, attribution, and permission boundaries discoverable without reading repository history.
- Keep reusable interface behavior in Goshtoso while identity-specific art direction, names, and legal guidance remain owned by Arai Hû.
- Treat generated pages, metadata, social previews, downloads, and redirects as one tested release surface.
